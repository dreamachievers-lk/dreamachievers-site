We apply for tuition fee discounts for you

Tuition is the biggest cost of studying abroad, so we work on bringing it down from the first day. Every student we work with is supported to apply for the tuition fee discounts available to them.

Ask about fee discounts

  • Discount applications

    We find the tuition fee discounts you can apply for, then prepare and submit the applications with you.

  • Requests to universities

    We contact universities to ask for fee reductions wherever they are possible.

  • Help for students with financial need

    We prepare your financial documents and statements and ask for need-based support on your behalf.

Discounts are decided by each university. We do everything possible to put you in the strongest position.
